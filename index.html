The four basics of care

☀

Light

Most houseplants want bright, indirect light — near a window, but out of harsh afternoon sun. Rotate pots monthly for even growth.

💧

Water

Push a finger into the soil. If the top 2–3 cm are dry, water thoroughly until it drains. Overwatering kills more plants than drought.

🪴

Soil & Pots

Always use pots with drainage holes. A chunky mix with bark and perlite keeps roots airy and healthy.

🌱

Feeding

Feed with a diluted liquid fertilizer every 4–6 weeks in spring and summer. Skip it in winter when growth slows.

Reading the leaves

Your plant is always telling you something.

SymptomLikely causeWhat to do
Yellow, soft lower leavesOverwateringLet soil dry out; check drainage.
Crispy brown tipsLow humidity or underwateringWater more consistently; group plants together.
Long, leggy stemsNot enough lightMove closer to a window; prune to encourage bushiness.
Pale or bleached patchesToo much direct sunShift out of harsh afternoon light.
Fine webbing, speckled leavesSpider mitesRinse leaves; wipe with insecticidal soap weekly.

Pro tip: Before bringing a new plant home, check whether it's safe for pets. Pothos, peace lilies, and ZZ plants are toxic if chewed by cats or dogs; spider plants are a pet-friendly choice.
